### 1. Elevator Pitch / Project Description
> **OpenSpace** is a civic tech web application that safely unlocks public school, university, and library facilities outside regular academic hours (evenings and weekends) for youth orchestras, sports clinics, neighborhood workshops, and community events.
> 
> Unlike generic venue booking software, OpenSpace is tailored to educational and civic institutions. It features an upfront **Rule Fit Evaluation Engine** that evaluates proposed events against safety policies (noise limits, custodial supervision, allowed activities), an **Automated Conflict Prevention Engine** that halts double-bookings, an interactive **Weekly Calendar Timeline View**, support for **Recurring Series Bookings** with batch availability checking, an **In-App Notification Center**, and **CSV/Printable Executive Usage Reports** for campus administrators.

---

### 2. Inspiration (The Problem We're Solving)
Every evening and weekend, tens of thousands of publicly funded classrooms, auditoriums, media labs, and gymnasiums sit locked and dark. Meanwhile, community organizations, non-profit youth sports leagues, and cultural groups are priced out of private commercial spaces or forced into subpar alternatives.

When educational institutions do rent spaces, administrative staff are overwhelmed by paper forms, liability worries, noise complaints, and accidental double-bookings. We built OpenSpace to eliminate these friction points with transparent policy enforcement, automated conflict math, and an intuitive two-sided workflow for organizers and school administrators.

---

### 3. What It Does

OpenSpace provides an end-to-end booking experience with a built-in **Demo Role Switcher** (`Community Organizer` ↔ `Institution Staff`):

#### For Community Organizers:
1. **Browse Vetted Campus Venues:** Filter by date, capacity, event category, price, and specialized facilities (e.g., sound booths, sprung floors, projectors).
2. **Instant Policy Fit Checking:** Before requesting a venue, organizers test their proposed hours, attendance, and noise level against the institution's specific rules. Clear diagnostic tags highlight whether the event is an approved fit or violates policies.
3. **Single & Recurring Bookings:** Submit individual requests or recurring series (weekly/bi-weekly for up to 8 sessions).
4. **Live Batch Availability Preview:** Real-time feedback shows which dates in a recurring series are available and flags any overlapping approved events.
5. **In-App Notifications:** Receive alerts when permit requests are approved, declined with staff feedback, or cancelled.

#### For Institution Staff & Facility Managers:
1. **Centralized Approval Dashboard:** Review all incoming requests in a structured **Table View** or an interactive **Weekly Timeline Grid**.
2. **Conflict Prevention:** Half-open time interval math `[start, end)` detects overlapping requests. Staff are strictly blocked from approving double-booked spaces.
3. **Facility Listing Management:** Add new facilities, update hourly rates and operating hours, toggle temporary availability, or remove listings.
4. **Export & Summary Reports:** One-click RFC 4180 CSV export for spreadsheets, plus a printable executive summary dialog (`@media print` optimized) detailing hours utilized and revenue generated by facility.

---

### 4. How We Built It

- **Frontend Core:** Built with **React 19** and strict **TypeScript** (`verbatimModuleSyntax: true`), bundled with **Vite 8** for sub-second hot reloading.
- **Design System:** Engineered a bespoke **Vanilla CSS design system** using fluid design tokens, modern typography, responsive CSS Grid and Flexbox layouts, accessible WCAG color contrast, and micro-animations.
- **Scheduling & Conflict Math:** Implemented interval evaluation algorithms ensuring back-to-back reservations (e.g. 17:00–19:00 and 19:00–21:00) remain valid while true overlaps are flagged instantly.
- **State & Storage:** Developed an isolated, persistent local storage service (`src/services/storage.ts`) with automatic fallback to memory and a one-click demo data reset.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance:** Backed by a comprehensive automated test suite in **Vitest** and **React Testing Library** with **100 tests passing across 15 test suites** and **0 lint errors** verified via **Oxlint**.

---

### 5. Challenges We Ran Into

1. **Complex Date & Recurrence Math:** Handling weekly and bi-weekly series across month and leap-year boundaries while performing concurrent conflict checks against existing database reservations.
2. **Half-Open Interval Scheduling:** Preventing false positives when one event ends at the exact minute another begins, while catching partial overlaps (e.g., 18:30–21:00 overlapping with 19:00–21:30).
3. **Dual-Role Demo Mode Architecture:** Providing a seamless experience where judges can test both the Organizer and Staff workflows in a single browser tab without requiring backend authentication or account setup.
4. **Strict React 19 Hook Ordering:** Ensuring dynamic modal dialogs and memoized conflict calculations adhered strictly to React's Rules of Hooks under varying render conditions.

---

### 6. Accomplishments That We're Proud Of

- **100/100 Tests Passing:** Unit and integration test coverage across all filtering, rule evaluation, booking lifecycles, calendar navigation, and notification workflows.
- **Zero Heavy UI Component Libraries:** Completely custom UI built with semantic HTML and CSS, resulting in an ultra-fast production bundle (under 390 kB).
- **Interactive Weekly Calendar Grid:** A responsive 7-day schedule timeline that rivals commercial booking platforms, complete with color-coded conflict warnings and quick approval popovers.
- **Real-World Civic Value:** Designed with genuine operational constraints that real school districts and civic facilities face daily.

---

### 7. What We Learned

- Designing for public sector users requires prioritizing **transparency and policy enforcement upfront** rather than dealing with disputes post-booking.
- Advanced accessibility patterns (skip links, ARIA live regions, keyboard drawer dismissal, and high-contrast color choices) make the platform significantly better for all users.
- How to structure extensible local simulation architectures for hackathon prototypes that can transition smoothly to backend APIs.

---

### 8. What's Next for OpenSpace

- **Stripe Connect Payment Processing:** Automated deposit handling and escrow release following custodial inspection.
- **IoT Smart Keycard & Smart Lock Integration:** Automatic generation of temporary digital PINs or QR codes for approved organizers.
- **Multi-District Multi-Tenancy:** Expanding to support unified county-wide school district portals.
- **Insurance & Non-Profit Verification:** Automated verification of 501(c)(3) tax status and general liability certificates.
